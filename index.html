Our History

Newbounds FC started in 2024 in its humble origins or Mdm Sarah's Geo Class. Back then, it was tactfully named FC Pondan Hamba, after its founder and captain, Eashvar Mahendran. Mdm Sarah played a pivotal role in nurturing the team's growth and telling us what a pondan hamba actually meant. After that, it was named the Masala Strikers after Messi Magic Masala Maggi. After that, the team continued to train and evolve. After many hours of debate, Julius consulted ChatGPT for guidance and gave us the name "Newbounds FC". We have lost every game since then.
